# Dodaios

Dodaios is a personal assistant that people talk to by voice and chat. This glossary fixes the words for what it is and how it presents itself.

## Presence

**Voice state**:
What the assistant is doing in a voice conversation at a given moment: idle, connecting, listening, thinking, speaking, paused or error.
_Avoid_: mode, status

**Look**:
A visual that takes the **Voice orb**'s place and animates according to the **Voice state** and the voice levels. Some looks ship with Dodaios; others are installed from the marketplace.
_Avoid_: skin, theme, avatar

**Voice orb**:
The default **Look**: a soft, cloud-filled sphere. It is used whenever no other **Look** is chosen or the chosen one fails.
_Avoid_: orb style, bubble

**Sentinel Ring**:
A **Look** drawn as a golden network of linked nodes around a glowing core. It swells while the assistant speaks, in proportion to how loud the voice is.
_Avoid_: neural network visual, hologram
